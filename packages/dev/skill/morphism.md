# Morphism

The interface is made of a substance. That substance has one surface, it is lit
from above, and every control is either pressed into it or lifted out of it.

This is the direction for a product whose users should feel that they are
touching something. It is the style where the shadow system is not one of the
decisions among many: it is the material, and without it the theme is a flat
theme with rounded corners.

## The type voice

One rounded or geometric sans, one family, one weight step above the body for
hierarchy, and a scale with a soft display step. The type is quiet, because in
this style the material is loud and the two together is too much.

Nothing is set in capitals except the smallest labels. A monospaced face can be
worth having for the numbers in a chart, and for nothing else.

## The palette direction

One hue, carried across the whole ramp. Neumorphism reads from shading rather
than from hue, so a single hue at several lightnesses gives the material more
room to be itself than a neutral ramp with a colour on top would.

The accent is used sparingly: the interactive state, the selected item, the one
thing on the screen that is live. The status colours stay inside the family, so
a green success is a green that belongs to this ramp and not imported from a
different theme.

## The shape

Very round. Twelve to twenty-four pixels on the surfaces, and the small controls
are pills. The radius ladder is the style's main character, and it has to have a
shape: the small step is clearly round rather than merely rounded, and the large
step is a pebble.

A flat theme with a sixteen pixel radius is a card with rounded corners. The
radius is necessary, but it is not sufficient.

## The depth

This is the style's defining decision, and it is not one shadow. Every surface
carries a matched pair: a light inner highlight from above and a dark outer
shadow below, both on the same axis. That pair is what makes a surface read as
extruded rather than as flat, and it is applied consistently to everything that
stands up.

The press state inverts the pair, so a pressed control sinks into the material
rather than shrinking. A neumorphic theme that does not invert on press is a
neumorphic theme that only works when nothing is pressed.

## The motion

Soft and physical, in the two-hundred-and-three-hundred-fifty-millisecond
region, with a gentle ease and no bounce. The press sinks, the release rises,
and the two are the same motion in opposite directions.

Motion is the material flexing. It should be felt as a change in pressure rather
than watched as an animation.

## The density

Comfortable to soft. Generous padding inside every container, rounded containers
with plenty of air around them, and controls large enough to be pressed with a
thumb, because in this style the touch is part of the point.

## The decisions that make it recognisable

1. **One hue's lightness ramp as the whole palette,** with the accent used
   sparingly so the shading can do the work.
2. **The matched shadow pair on every surface,** light above and dark below, on
   the same axis, and inverted on press.
3. **A very round radius ladder, with pills on the small controls.**

## How it drifts into the house style

It drifts the moment the surfaces go flat, because the shadows are not a
decoration in this style, they are the material. It drifts when the pair becomes
a single drop shadow, which turns extrusion into elevation and the theme into
every other theme. And it drifts when the palette picks up a second hue, because
a monochrome ramp with a coloured shadow is no longer one substance.

The tell is the shadow count. One shadow, and the clay is a card.
